# Notices

## Upstream
shangOS is a fork of **Luma TV** by 0xProbabillity (https://github.com/0xProbabillity/luma-tv),
v2.4 (versionCode 26), forked at commit `8ce6838`, used under the MIT licence. The original copyright
notice is kept in `LICENSE`, as the licence requires. The upstream remote is kept as `upstream` in git.

Changes made here are listed in `CHANGELOG.md`.

Typeface: Inter (Regular and Medium, subset to Latin with fontTools; the static instances were made from the variable font), Copyright
2020 The Inter Project Authors, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`res/raw/license_ofl.txt`). Not modified except by
subsetting and instancing.

Upstream was last checked against Luma 2.5 (`1c1bc52`). Taken from it: the keyboard window setting. The weather city picker is this
project's own, built on the same idea but with a list inside the app (see Data below). Not taken: Luma 2.5's automatic city from the TV's
internet address (GeoJS, ipwho.is), its online city search (Open-Meteo geocoding), and the promo-demo restyle (a film tool).

## Not carried over
The upstream author's donation addresses, QR codes, contact links and "Support the author" screen were
removed. They belong to the upstream author and must not appear in this fork.

## Data and services
- Time zone to city data: IANA time zone database `zone.tab` (public domain).
- The list of cities in Settings, City (about 23,000 towns and cities of 15,000 people or more): GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org, CC BY 4.0), as
  packaged by the community package all-the-cities (MIT). `res/raw/cities.txt` is generated by `tools/cities.js`, not written by hand.
- Weather (only if the user turns it on): Open-Meteo.com, data under CC BY 4.0. "Weather data by Open-Meteo.com".

The in-app list (Settings, About, Licences) shows the same credits with full licence texts.
